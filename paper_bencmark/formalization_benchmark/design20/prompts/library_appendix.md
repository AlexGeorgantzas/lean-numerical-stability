
## NumStability treatment condition

You have a frozen, complete NumStability snapshot in addition to Mathlib, and
you inherit a one-time task-neutral orientation conversation. Its core
floating-point and gamma definitions need not be rediscovered from scratch.

`LIBRARY_API.md` offers ranked starting suggestions only. It is **not** an
exhaustive list or a permission boundary. You may inspect `/library-index`,
search `/library/NumStability` read-only, inspect exact declaration types, and
import any compatible module from the frozen snapshot. Search first for the
paper's algorithm representation, floating-point model, and relevant generic
components; if a suggested card does not fit, look for another. Try to reuse
the library where doing so preserves the paper's full domain and conclusion.
Do not narrow the task to fit a declaration or mistake a related bound for
the target. Task-time search and all formalization work are measured.
