# Prospective Pilot 29 proof-required development protocol

Status: ten-task admission passed; no Pilot 29 measurement has started. This
document records the intended analysis before any Pilot 29 measured task.
Prior Pilot 26–28 outcomes are known, so Pilot 29 is explicitly
outcome-aware exploratory development, never a held-out effect estimate.

## Scientific question and outcome order

Compare fresh N (Lean/Mathlib) and warm L (the same environment plus one
hash-frozen NumStability snapshot) on paper-sourced tasks with independently
audited faithful statements followed by complete kernel-checked proof
attempts. The primary descriptive formalization outcome is the size of each
condition's final, newly-authored Lean **statement** candidate when both
statements pass the audit. A separate proof outcome is the size of the final
proof candidate only when **both** conditions prove their frozen statements;
proof success and attrition are reported for every task. This distinction was
fixed before Pilot 29 after Pilot 28 exposed a faithfully audited but false
overgeneralized rounding model. Preserve every task's statement/proof status
and all failures. Direct elaborated reach of a relevant NumStability declaration is
the treatment-uptake check, not an automatic success criterion. Report
contestant-system wall time and net-new tokens next, with task-time searching
included; search-time-excluded figures are diagnostic only and must never
replace the inclusive measurements. Report raw source lines as such; a
secondary nonblank/noncomment code-line count is generated by the frozen
post-processing script for both statement and proved candidates.

## Treatment and isolation

The source PDF, target packet, common prompt prefix, formalizer model
(GPT-6 Sol/high), Lean/Mathlib, task order, audit rubric, and 8-logical-CPU /
24-GiB/no-swap lane envelope are fixed and hash-checked. L receives only the
library appendix, full read-only NumStability source/oleans, and a one-time
task-neutral warm orientation root. The initial orientation cost is recorded
separately; task-specific searching remains on the contestant clock. Neither
condition receives a target theorem skeleton, proof, private admission
candidate, retrieved oracle packet, or competitor output. No automatic
retrieval/routing tool or hidden task-specific declaration shortlist is used.
Three disjoint hardware lanes run independent tasks concurrently after the
first-pair review; an off-benchmark admission check may use a separate idle
lane but must not be counted as a contestant run.

For each attempt the formalizer produces one compiling statement with one
target `sorry`; compilation and proof-integrity checks precede a fresh,
condition-blind, candidate-specific, multi-role faithfulness audit. Its
binary decision accepts equivalence or faithful strengthening but rejects
missing source-domain coverage. The audit retains per-dependency records,
the 16-point semantic checklist, blind translation, direct and round-trip
judgments, and conditional adjudication. Neutral failure feedback returns to
the same conversation; up to four statement submissions share one cumulative
contestant time budget. Only after a faithful statement is frozen does that
same condition attempt a complete proof in its own conversation, with up to
four submissions and a cumulative 3,600-second proof-turn limit. An accepted
proof must compile without `sorry` or unsafe axioms and preserve the audited
statement's semantic hash. Audit/validation time and tokens are logged but
excluded from contestant cost.

## Corpus and stopping rule

The hash-frozen ten-task schedule and declared strata are in
`CORPUS_10.json` and `PROSPECTIVE_10.md`. They were selected after prior
outcomes, partly to test substantial algorithm/error reuse and partly to
retain source variety. Four finite-format/polynomial cases have direct but
foundational reach; H20-8 reaches a backward-error interface without an
FPModel. They must not be described as six/ten strict FP-algorithm tasks.
CAST08-PROP3.1 and the improved CAST08-PROP3.2 require separately compiling,
source-faithful private candidates with actual recursive-sum/dot-product
reach. Private candidates are admission evidence only, never contestant
inputs. The full corpus is launched only after its source, collision,
candidate, and exact-snapshot gates pass. The first measured pair is reviewed
before the remaining tasks; a protocol or infrastructure defect stops that
pilot and requires a new identity. Bad scientific outcomes are preserved, not
silently rerun, relabeled, or dropped. Once the reviewed protocol is sound,
the entire predeclared schedule runs and all attrition is reported.
