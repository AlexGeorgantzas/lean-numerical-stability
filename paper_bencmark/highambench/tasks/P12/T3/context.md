## Local context and statement

ThreeProduct first expands three exact two-factor products into four terms,
merges the two middle errors by FastTwoSum, and adds the merge error to the
last product error exactly. The target states this complete certificate-level
composition and proves that the three returned values sum to `x1*x2*x3`.
