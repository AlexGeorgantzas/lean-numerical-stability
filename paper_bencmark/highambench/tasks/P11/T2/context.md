## Local context and statement

Starting from `QR=A+ΔA`, the paper substitutes `Q=(A+ΔA)R⁻¹` into
`I-QᵀQ`. It obtains an exact inverse-conjugated expression containing the
normal-equations residual and three perturbation products. The target records
that identity for finite real square matrices and a certified right inverse.
