## Local context and statement

Theorem 1 bounds the loss of orthogonality by the squared inverse-factor norm
times the normal-equation and factorization-error budgets. The target replaces
all first-order and `O(epsilon²)` bookkeeping by explicit finite budgets
`e`, `a`, `d`, and `rho`, derives the paper's exact defect identity, and proves
the completed bound `rho² (e + 2ad + d²)`.
