# P18-T3 context

Source: Method 4s3pC and the two displayed error regimes on PDF page 18 (article page 18 of 20), with their numerical interpretation continuing on page 19.

For a well-behaved perturbation `τ`, the paper gives `O(Δt³) + O(ε Δt³)`; for a nonsmooth perturbation it gives `O(Δt³) + O(ε Δt²)`. The target turns both into exact finite-vector bounds and proves that, for `0 ≤ Δt ≤ 1` and `ε ≥ 0`, the smooth envelope is no larger than the nonsmooth one.

The final ordering is an explicit finite strengthening that makes the paper's two asymptotic regimes directly comparable.
