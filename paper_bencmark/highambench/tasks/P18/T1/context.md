# P18-T1 context

Source: equation (3.3) and the paragraph immediately below it on PDF page 5 (article page 5 of 20).

The paper separates the one-step Runge--Kutta error into the approximation error of the scheme, `E_sch`, and the mixed-precision perturbation error, `E_per`, writing `E = E_sch + E_per`. The target is the exact finite-dimensional Euclidean-norm inequality underlying that decomposition.

This exact inequality replaces the paper's asymptotic notation with explicit vectors and is independent of any stability assumption.
