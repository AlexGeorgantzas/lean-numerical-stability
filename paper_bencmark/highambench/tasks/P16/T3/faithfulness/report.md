## Decision

Primary evidence confirms that Theorem 6.3 is an algorithm-specific mixed-precision rounding-error result about explicitly defined errors. The Lean declaration is instead a universally quantified exact recurrence lemma whose central contraction behavior is assumed. The paper supplies, at most, an intended algorithm-generated instance after several additional identifications; it does not imply the full Lean statement, and the Lean statement cannot derive the paper's algorithmic result. Thus the target is substantively weaker but logically different, both implication verdicts are no, and the final classification is not-faithful-different.

## Implications

- **Lean implies paper:** `no`. The Lean proposition assumes exact one-step contraction and derives conditional geometric bounds for arbitrary sequences. It cannot derive that mixed-precision restarted MGS-GMRES satisfies those recurrences, cannot identify the sequences with the paper's normalized backward and relative forward errors, and contains none of the algorithmic or floating-point hypotheses needed for Theorem 6.3.
- **Paper implies lean:** `no`. The paper proves a result only for errors generated by its specified algorithm, with nonsingular A, the actual A inverse, qualitative constants, and mixed-precision kernel assumptions. It does not establish the Lean declaration's full universal exact statement for arbitrary A, unrelated Ainv, arbitrary c, arbitrary real sequences, and every exact factor q<1. At most it motivates one intended algorithm-generated instance after additional identifications and formalization choices.

## Findings

- **critical / algorithm-and-quantifier-substitution:** The main numerical-analysis conclusion is replaced by a recurrence premise and an induction consequence.
- **critical / error-definitions:** The declaration proves no property of either paper-defined error notion.
- **critical / inverse-and-conditioning:** The purported condition number, contraction factor, and forward floor can be unrelated to the source quantities and admit trivial non-paper instances.
- **major / floating-point-model:** The mixed-precision execution model responsible for the theorem is absent.
- **major / relations-constants-and-indexing:** The parameter dependence, admissible regime, hidden constants, and approximation semantics are changed.
- **major / nonvacuity-and-applicability:** The apparent universality is reduced applicability under assumed behavior, not genuine strengthening of the paper theorem.
- **note / inherited-paper-hypothesis:** A literal formalization must address this inherited condition, but doing so would not repair the independent Lean mismatches.
