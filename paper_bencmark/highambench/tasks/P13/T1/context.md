## Local context and statement

At a fixed evaluation point, the interpolation value is the finite sum of the
Lagrange basis values times the data. The componentwise condition number is
the absolute-value sum divided by the magnitude of that interpolation value.
The target states that this ratio is at least one whenever the value is
nonzero.
