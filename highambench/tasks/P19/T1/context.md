# P19-T1 context

Source: equation (C.8) on physical PDF page 42 (printed article page 41 of 46).

In the right-preconditioned GMRES proof, the paper relates the stored basis product and its matrix-product correction by an additive perturbation. Equation (C.8) brackets the norm of the corrected product between the stored-product norm minus the correction norm and their sum. The target selects the exact upper inequality.

The target uses explicit finite vectors and the paper-scoped Euclidean norm; it introduces no asymptotic constant or GMRES convergence assumption.
