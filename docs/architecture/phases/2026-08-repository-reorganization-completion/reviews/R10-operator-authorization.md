# R10 temporary operator authorization

The primary-human retains ownership of the immutable `codex-lane` and temporarily adds
`claude-local` as a second lane operator solely for B0012/R10 planning stewardship, activated
worker execution, delivery reporting, and the immutable delivery ref. B0012 records
`claude-local`; B0011/R09 remains exclusively `claude-local` in `claude-lane`.

The immutable C0000 scope freeze assigns all eighteen R10 owners to `codex-lane`, and that
assignment is not editable, so operating the wave requires this authorization rather than a
lane reassignment.

This authorization expires at C0007 acceptance or when B0012 becomes terminal, whichever
comes first. It grants no B0011, integration, shared-path, main-push, checkpoint acceptance,
or retirement authority. Those authorities remain with `primary-human`. No worker ref or
worktree may be created before the exact planned-control Lean CI is green, and no
implementation may begin before a later activation-control commit is green.
