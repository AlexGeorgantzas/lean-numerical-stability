# W04 declaration routing

W04 splits the 29 C0006 owners at complete Lean-command boundaries.  The
hash-pinned `PRIVATE_CLOSURE.tsv` decides historical retention; the remaining
commands are routed by `GENERATE_MIGRATION.py` only beneath B0008's 42 reviewed
production prefixes.

## Reusable layer

Reusable commands are grouped under the reviewed underdetermined-system
families for minimum-norm specifications and pseudoinverses, solvers,
componentwise and normwise perturbation, conditioning, complementary
projectors, QR foundations, Givens replay and backward error, corrected and
rounded modified Gram--Schmidt, full-row-rank stability, and the five
seminormal-equations API families.

The generator reads the hash-pinned P0009 projection extracted from the full
C0006 format-2 graph and closes the source side upward over signature and
proof dependencies.  Consequently, a reusable leaf
has neither a direct nor a transitive dependency on `NumStability.Source`.
The independent `CHECK_STATIC.py` traversal verifies the resulting import
graph rather than relying on naming alone.

## Chapter 21 source layer

Printed equations 21.1--21.11, Lemma 21.2, Theorems 21.1, 21.3, and 21.4,
the Section 21.3 method comparison, the Problem 19.12 correction, and source
closures are placed only beneath their exact B0008 Chapter 21 prefixes.
Commands whose unchanged statements depend on an existing Source declaration
join the corresponding exact source closure; they are never forced into the
reusable tier by changing a statement or proof.

`NumStability.Source.Higham.Chapter21.Equation04.Pseudoinverse` is an
import-only exact source endpoint for the integrator-owned equality-constrained
least-squares consumer.

## Historical compatibility

All 29 historical paths remain.  Owners with a private reverse closure retain
those complete commands and import every canonical leaf receiving one of
their moved commands.  Owners without retained declarations are import-only
shims.  No canonical module imports a W04 historical facade.

`DECLARATION_ROUTES.tsv` is the authoritative declaration-by-declaration
ledger; `RETENTION.tsv` is the authoritative owner/facade summary.
